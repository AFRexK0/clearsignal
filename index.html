How it works

A close. A flip. A message.

Each enabled pair and timeframe is watched on closed candles only. When the live rule changes direction, Telegram gets a new signal. Same-direction repeats stay silent so the channel does not spam.

  1. 01

    Closed candles

    Unfinished bars are dropped so live flips match the backtest. 5m, 15m, 1h, and 4h each run on their own clock.

  2. 02

    Direction flip

    You are long or short until the opposite print. A CLOSE alert includes leveraged result at the suggested leverage, then the new side.

  3. 03

    Tiered Telegram

    BTC and ETH go to Free, Premium, and Elite. Alts go to Premium and Elite; Free receives a teaser, not the entry.

  4. 04

    You execute

    Entry, suggested leverage, and candle time are in the post. No API keys. No exchange connection. Risk stays with you.

Capabilities

Built like infrastructure, not a hype feed.

Every live rule is gated on held-out history after fees and slippage. The site, the bots, and the channels share one product language: hold until opposite.

One live idea per symbol and timeframe. The next notification is a true reversal, not another ping in the same direction.

Majors first, then liquid alts ranked by 24h quote volume. Stables, leveraged tokens, and gold wrappers are excluded.

Close + PnL

Two systems, one format

AI where it is approved. Rules where they clear the gate.

Posts look the same on purpose. Underneath, production AI and the indicator engine keep separate state so they never overwrite each other.

LSTM

Neural path

Sequence models on OHLCV features. Only profitability-approved checkpoints are allowed live — unprofitable training runs do not overwrite production.

  • Feature windows floored so 4h/1d vol and z-scores do not collapse to zero.
  • Held-out evaluation with fees and slippage before a model may go live.
  • Per-symbol, per-interval checkpoints (for example BTCUSDT 1h).
  • Same Signal object as the rest of the stack: entry, leverage, candle time.
  • Not financial advice and not an execution bot.

Rules

Indicator path

Adaptive lookbacks in market time (~6h–12d), ATR floors from each series, Supertrend width from vol, Donchian on slower bars.

  • Families: momentum, Supertrend, Donchian (1h+). Mean-reversion is not used live after it failed OOS.
  • Inner validation, then a frozen held-out test. Only gate_pass rules are enabled.
  • Live kline cap 1000 bars — windows are designed to still fit Binance.
  • First start writes a cursor and does not dump history into Telegram.
  • Independent cursors and live-signal state under indicator_system/state.

Coverage

Majors on every screen. Alts where they earned it.

Enabled rules are the ones that passed verification — not a hand-waved watchlist. Timeframes and pairs can be expanded after a new gate pass.

FAQ

Straight answers

Is this financial advice?

No. Signals are educational market commentary. You are responsible for fills, risk, and losses. Past results do not predict the future.

Does the bot trade my exchange?

No. There is no exchange API, no copy-trading hook, and no withdrawal permission. You place orders yourself.

Why can 4h stay quiet for days?

Hold-until-opposite only posts on a direction change. A 4h trend can run many candles without a flip. 15m and 1h print more often when those rules are enabled.

How do Stripe payments activate access?

Stripe Payment Links are hosted by Stripe. After success you land on a thank-you page and open Telegram. Paste or message the plan you paid so the operator / bot can issue the invite. Links are configured in website/assets/js/config.js — until they are set, use USDT.

Which network for crypto?

USDT on Polygon (chain id 137), contract 0xc213…e8f, six decimals. Other chains will not verify. Gas is paid in POL.

Can I cancel?

There is no subscription auto-charge. Access lasts 7 or 30 days from a successful activation, then you simply do not renew.

What is the difference vs random Telegram calls?

Indicator rules must pass a held-out profitability gate after modeled fees. Live state is per symbol+timeframe. First start does not replay history into the channel.

Do you store my seed phrase?

Never. Wallet connect only requests a USDT transfer. The site never asks for a seed, private key, or exchange password.

Risk

Crypto assets are volatile. Leverage can wipe an account. ClearSignal, its operators, and this website do not promise profit, do not manage your funds, and are not a broker or advisor. Only use capital you can afford to lose. Verify addresses, networks, and Stripe URLs yourself.
